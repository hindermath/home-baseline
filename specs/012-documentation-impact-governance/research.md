# Research

## Decision 1: Four exact outcomes

The four intake outcomes are exhaustive for current work, justified no-change,
generated output and bounded later work. Adding synonyms would weaken parity.

## Decision 2: Structural validator

The validator checks fields, decisions and risk boundaries. Semantic accuracy
continues to require review evidence; plain text search cannot prove truth.

## Decision 3: No new preset

This is a Level-0 governance baseline. Promotion requires reproduction across
providers or repositories after D3, so D2 does not create a preset.
