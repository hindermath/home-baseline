# Acceptance Contract: Intake Sequencing Governance

The feature is accepted when:

- the six canonical commands exist exactly once per supported agent surface;
- manifest and receipt validators agree across Bash and PowerShell;
- positive synthetic and Legacy-Adoption fixtures pass;
- malformed paths, identities, hashes, orders, roots, edges, cycles, lifecycle,
  authority, archive, and tombstone evidence fail closed;
- read, status, and next are proven read-only;
- no command modifies intake content or starts a downstream command;
- the eleven-preset profile is optional and existing profiles are unchanged;
- publication ZIP, checksum, fleet evidence, and community submission exist.
